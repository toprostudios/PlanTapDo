# 📄 PlanTapDo — Non-Technical Product Overview & Export Report

## Executive Summary

**PlanTapDo** is an all-in-one productivity, time-blocking, and team execution platform designed to replace fractured workflows. Modern professionals currently rely on 4 to 5 separate tools—**Todoist** for tasks, **Google Calendar** for time blocks, **Notion** for project notes, and **Toggl Track** for time tracking. 

PlanTapDo unifies these workflows into a single seamless experience on **Web** and **iOS**. Users can capture tasks using natural language, schedule them onto interactive multi-day timeline grids, write rich Notion-style notes per category, track active execution with real-time timers, and provide managers with side-by-side team visibility.

---

## 🌟 Key Product Pillar Highlights

### 1. 🗓️ Intelligent Calendar Scheduling (Google Calendar Equivalent)
- **Multi-Day Timelines**: Switch effortlessly between 1-Day, 3-Day, and 7-Day Weekly views.
- **Drag-and-Drop Adjustment**: Click and drag tasks across hours to dynamically adjust planned execution times.
- **Overlap & Travel Time Intelligence**: Allows overlapping commitments and automatically calculates travel times between locations (e.g., `HQ Office ➔ Gym`), inserting visual transit blocks on calendar timelines.

### 2. ⚡ Frictionless Task Management (Todoist Equivalent)
- **Natural Language Quick Add**: Type standard English commands like `"Sprint review tomorrow at 10am #work @HQ Office !high 45m"` to automatically populate dates, times, categories, locations, priorities, and durations.
- **Subtask Checklists**: Break complex objectives into clear subtasks with individual completion toggles.
- **Descriptive Deadlines & Metadata**: Attach descriptive deadline notes (which do not distort calendar positioning), reminders, labels, and priority badges (`Urgent`, `High`, `Medium`, `Low`).

### 3. 📓 Document Canvases & Kanban Boards (Notion Equivalent)
- **Category Document Canvas**: Every category contains a dedicated Notion-style page header with customizable cover banners, emoji badges, and full text editing.
- **Slash Block Toolbar**: 1-click text formatting tools (`Heading 1`, `Heading 2`, `Task Checkbox`, `Bullet List`, `Quote`, `Code Block`).
- **Kanban Board View**: View task backlogs as drag-and-drop status columns (`To Do`, `In Progress`, `Completed`, `Skipped`).
- **Color Swatch System**: Curated 8-color palette swatches and custom pickers to color-code categories across all cards and calendar blocks.

### 4. ⏱️ Active Time Tracking & Analytics (Toggl Track Equivalent)
- **Live Floating Timer Bar**: Persistent top bar showing active task title, category badge, location, and real-time digital `HH:MM:SS` counter with a pulsing status light.
- **Toggl Time Analytics Modal**: Comprehensive analytics dashboard displaying total tracked hours, planned budgets, efficiency ratios (%), category time breakdowns, and location breakdowns.

### 5. 👥 Team & Manager Workspace View
- **Multi-Person Side-by-Side View**: Managers can monitor multiple team members simultaneously in a side-by-side grid.
- **Active Task Monitor (`🔴 ACTIVE NOW`)**: Instantly inspect what each team member is working on right now.
- **Lined-Up Schedules**: Chronologically sorted list of upcoming tasks lined up for each team member.
- **Workload Capacity Gauges**: Visual capacity bar showing planned workload percentage vs daily limits.
- **Manager Task Dispatcher**: 1-click tool for assigning new tasks or re-assigning existing tasks between team members.

### 6. 👤 User Accounts & Multi-Account Switcher
- **1-Click Account Switcher**: Switch between Work, Personal, and Team Workspaces without logging out.
- **Cloud Sync Status**: Real-time cloud sync status indicator and workspace metrics.

## 🚀 Target Audience & Value Proposition

- **For Individual Professionals**: Eliminates context switching between task apps, calendars, and timers. Everything you plan is automatically reflected in your calendar and tracked in your analytics.
- **For Managers & Team Leads**: Provides immediate real-time visibility into team member workloads, active tasks, and upcoming commitments without needing status update meetings.
- **Cross-Platform Parity**: Identical feature set on Web and iOS ensures seamless productivity on desktop or on the go.
