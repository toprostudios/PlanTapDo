# PlanTapDo Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 27, 2026  
**Last updated:** August 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Topro Industry Inc** ("PlanTapDo," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the PlanTapDo iPhone and iPad app and contact our support service. Topro Industry Inc is responsible for the information described in this Policy.

## The short version

PlanTapDo version 1 is a local-only personal planner. Your tasks, categories, schedules, timer history, reports, preferences, and Off Time settings are stored on your device. PlanTapDo does not provide accounts, cloud synchronization, or server-hosted task storage in version 1.

We do not sell personal information, show third-party advertising, track you across other companies' apps or websites, or use third-party product analytics in PlanTapDo version 1.

## Information stored on your device

PlanTapDo stores the information you create or configure in the app, including task titles and notes, categories, scheduled times, task durations, timer sessions, recurrence settings, reports, appearance preferences, and Off Time settings. This information is used only to provide the planner on your device.

PlanTapDo may schedule local notifications when you enable them. Those notifications are created and delivered through iOS on your device; PlanTapDo does not send your task data to a PlanTapDo server to deliver them.

You control this local information. You can edit or delete tasks and categories in the app. Removing the app generally removes its local data from that device. If you enable Apple device backups, Apple may retain an encrypted backup according to your Apple account settings and Apple's policies; PlanTapDo does not control or access those backups.

## Purchases

PlanTapDo may offer an in-app purchase that unlocks unlimited categories. Apple processes App Store purchases and payment information. We do not receive or store your payment-card or bank-account details.

The app uses Apple's StoreKit framework on your device to check purchase entitlements and restore eligible purchases. PlanTapDo version 1 does not send StoreKit transaction identifiers or task data to a PlanTapDo server.

## Support communications

If you email us for support, privacy, accessibility, billing, or technical help, we receive the information you choose to include in your message, such as your email address and the details needed to answer your request. Please do not send passwords, payment-card numbers, or other sensitive information.

We use support communications only to respond, troubleshoot, maintain required records, protect our rights, and comply with law. We retain them only as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes.

## How we disclose information

Because PlanTapDo version 1 does not receive your task data, we do not disclose that data to advertising networks, data brokers, or a PlanTapDo cloud service. We may disclose support correspondence only when reasonably necessary to use service providers that help us operate support, comply with law, respond to valid legal process, protect people or our rights, or complete a business transaction permitted by law.

## Your choices and privacy requests

There is no PlanTapDo account or cloud vault to access, export, or delete in version 1. You can manage local task data directly in the app or remove the app from your device. If you have a question about a support message or want to make a privacy request, contact our Privacy Officer using the details below.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information we hold in support correspondence, withdraw consent, or complain to a privacy regulator. We may ask for information reasonably necessary to verify a request.

## Children

PlanTapDo does not create accounts or knowingly collect personal information through the app. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through support, contact us and we will address the request as required by applicable law.

## Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy to reflect changes to PlanTapDo, our practices, or legal requirements. We will post the updated version and revise the "Last updated" date. If we later add accounts, cloud synchronization, analytics, or other material data practices, we will update this Policy and the App Store privacy disclosure before those practices begin.
