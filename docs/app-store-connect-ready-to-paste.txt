DESCRIPTION
-----------
PlanTapDo turns your task list into a live, time-blocked daily plan that adapts as your day changes.

Build a schedule that adjusts with you
• Turn tasks into visual time blocks on your daily calendar.
• Assign a start time and duration, or let untimed work fill available space.
• When a task is delayed, its block moves forward automatically instead of becoming a stale reminder.
• Keep fixed-time commitments in place while flexible work adjusts around them.
• Push and rearrange tasks directly as plans change.

Track the work you actually do
• Start a task to run its built-in focus timer.
• Compare planned duration with the time you actually spent.
• Review completed tasks and focus history to understand where your day went.

Protect your time
• Set Off Time so tasks do not spill into sleep or personal hours.
• Organize work into customizable categories.
• Create daily, weekly, monthly, or selected-weekday recurring tasks.
• Recurring tasks appear only when they are due, without filling your list with future duplicates.

Private by design
Your tasks, schedules, categories, timer history, and preferences stay on your device. Version 1 has no PlanTapDo account, cloud sync, advertising, or third-party analytics.

PlanTapDo is free to use with up to two categories. PlanTapDo Advanced unlocks unlimited categories through a monthly or annual auto-renewing subscription.
